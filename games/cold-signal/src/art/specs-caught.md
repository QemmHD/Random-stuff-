# Art specs — CAUGHT mode (cases C1 "Zero Threat", C2 "Mystery Man", C3 "Fantasy League")

Mode framing: the player IS the partner, reading their partner's phone at 2–3 a.m. Second person throughout. The handler is **Dez, your best friend** (`av_cg_bff`), who is panicky, loyal and swears. Tone: M-rated adult comedy that gets steamy through craft and suggestion: candlelight, rumpled sheets, borrowed hoodies, lipstick on things. Each case lands a real gut-punch.

**Hard content limits for every image:** adults only (every person 25+). No nipples, areolas, genitals or buttocks crack. Nothing depicts a sex act. "Steamy" means implied: covered bodies, robes, towels, oversized hoodies, silhouettes, sheets, steam and candlelight. Lingerie shots are mirror-selfie or catalogue style, with arms, robes or angle keeping them non-explicit. No real brands, logos, songs or people. All signage below is invented.

Sizes: photos portrait 600x800 / landscape 800x600 · covers 600x800 · wallpapers 600x1300 · maps 800x600 · avatars 200x200.

Palette: warm phone-flash interiors at night (amber lamps, candle orange, streetlight sodium) against cold blue phone-screen glow. Hidden-album shots are moodier: steam, low light and red/pink accents.

Reused existing art: `av_unknown` (C1 "Dr. J 🦷 (lab)", C3 "Pickle Coach (ignore)").
