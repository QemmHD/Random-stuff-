# Art specs — Case 02 "Dead Air"

Palette & mood: sodium-orange streetlight, deep indigo night, wet brick, warm fairy-light amber inside the Coop. Signature accent: **Cass's bright yellow raincoat** (keep the exact same yellow, ~#F2C318, everywhere it appears — it is the case's key visual clue). Owl motif on Jonah's things.

Sizes: photos portrait 600x800 / landscape 800x600 · cover 600x800 · wallpapers 600x1300 · map 800x600 · avatars 200x200.

Reused existing art: `av_unknown` (the burner number). Handler uses `av_ines`.

## Cover

- `cover_case2` (600x800) — The Carraway Mill at night seen from the parking lot: a hulking brick textile mill, a lit window and a homemade antenna mast on the roof, an iron fire stair zig-zagging down the side. A thin sound-wave line across the sky goes flat in the middle (dead air). Title area top: "DEAD AIR". A tiny yellow figure on a bicycle leaving the frame, bottom right.

## Wallpapers

- `wall_c2_lock` (600x1300) — **PASSCODE CLUE.** Close-up of a hand-painted wooden station sign on brick, string fairy lights above. Painted text, legible: **"THE OWL HOURS"**, below it **"88.1 FM"**, below that **"FRI · MIDNIGHT–4"**. The **"88.1"** is struck through with a strip of red gaffer tape. A taped index card in the corner reads, handwritten: **"MOVING UP THE DIAL ↑"**. Leave the top third relatively calm (clock overlays it).
- `wall_c2_home` (600x1300) — City skyline at 3 a.m. from a rooftop, the mill's antenna mast in the foreground with a small owl silhouette perched on the crossbar. Rain streaks, indigo and orange.
