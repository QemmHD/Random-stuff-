# Art specs — Case 09 "Funeral Potatoes"

Palette & mood: Harlow Falls in October, a faded lake town with old money and new car washes. Mahogany, black funeral suits, white lilies and gold rings against the neon chaos of a taco-shaped food truck (orange shell, green lettuce trim, a cheese-yellow awning). At night: motel neon, sodium lamps and lake-black water. Tone: a mob farce. The Pettibones look like a crime-family portrait painted by someone who works at a car wash.

Sizes: photos portrait 600x800 / landscape 800x600 · cover 600x800 · wallpapers 600x1300 · map 800x600 · avatars 200x200.

Reused existing art: `av_unknown` (the Lakeshore Motor Inn courtesy phone, (555) 302-0199). Handler uses `av_ines`.

Recurring objects:
- **The Munchie Wagon** is a step van whose body is shaped like a giant hard-shell taco (orange shell, painted lettuce and tomato along the top). The serving window sits in the "filling". Lettering: **"THE MUNCHIE WAGON — YOU'RE GONNA NEED A NAP"**.
- **The Wishing Well** is a white satin box about the size of a microwave, with a gold slot and a gold script plaque, **"IN LOVING MEMORY — THE WISHING WELL"**, stuffed with cream envelopes.
- **Donna's white SUV** is a big white luxury SUV with a vanity plate, **"DONNA 1"**.
- **Big Lou** is 68, enormous, with a bald head, a white moustache and pinky rings. He wears a white terry bathrobe in the motel scenes.
- **Gordon Fisk** is thin and pale, with a black suit, a comb-over and an expression of permanent apology.

## Wallpapers

- `c9_wall_lock` (600x1300). **PASSCODE CLUE.** Inside the taco truck's cab at night, looking at the dashboard. Taped to the dash is an old school calculator. Its 7-segment LCD clearly shows **"5318008"**. A yellow sticky note under it in marker reads: **"BOOBIES — still funny — D, age 15"**. Two more sticky notes on the dash:
  - **"0.7734 = hELLO"**
  - **"0208 = BOZO (spatula)"** (this shows that a 2 flips into a Z).
  - Through the windshield the truck's own reversed awning lettering is barely visible. A dangling air freshener shaped like a pot leaf, a bobblehead taco, and a parking ticket.
  - Logic: Mom's lock notification gives the first truck name, **SIZZLE**. Upside down on a calculator S=5, I=1, Z=2, L=7, E=3, and flipping reverses the order (5318008 reads BOOBIES). SIZZLE is typed as **372215**.
- `c9_wall_home` (600x1300): The taco truck at golden hour at the farmers market, the line twenty people long. Dewey leans out of the window holding a tray of tots like a newborn.
