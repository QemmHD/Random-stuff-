# Art specs — Case 08 "Ball Pit"

Palette & mood: a sun-bleached suburban strip mall at night. Sodium-orange parking-lot light, flickering neon (vape shop green, nail salon pink), cheap fluorescent interiors and the candy colours of a kids' party venue (red/yellow/blue plastic balls) under police-blue light. Tone: Fargo-style deadpan. Everything is slightly too bright, slightly tacky and slightly sad. Dale's world is black, gold and glitter: shiny polyester belts, a gi with **"HUPP"** bedazzled across the back, and a banner misspelled **"HUPP'S FREESTLYE KARATE — HOME OF THE DRAGON KICK"**.

Sizes: photos portrait 600x800 / landscape 800x600 · cover 600x800 · wallpapers 600x1300 · map 800x600 · avatars 200x200.

Reused existing art: `av_unknown` (Dolores, the laundromat attendant at (555) 448-2091). Handler uses `av_ines`.

Recurring objects:
- **The 10th Dan belts** are glossy black polyester with gold embroidery ("10th DAN GRANDMASTER") and a white printed care label, legible where it shows: **"NOVELTY DISPLAY BELT · 100% POLYESTER · NOT FOR LOAD BEARING"**.
- **The Iron Lotus hoodie** is black with a big white lotus logo and the words **"IRON LOTUS MARTIAL ARTS"** across the chest.
- **Dale** is 47, 5'6", with a greying ponytail, a goatee, a slight paunch, a bedazzled black gi and a "NO MERCY" headband. He wears a black ski mask on the night.
- **Corey** is 31, very thin and tall, with a patchy moustache, a white gi with a yellow belt, and a green pet-store polo when he's off duty. He drives a green hatchback with a **"PAWS & CLAWS PET SUPPLY"** sticker.
- **The ball pit** (Jumpin' Jax) is square, knee-deep in red/yellow/blue balls, with a **steel frame under thin blue padding** on its rim. A square ceiling hatch sits directly above it.
